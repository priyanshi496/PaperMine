# Enterprise Procurement Policy

**Last Updated**: 2026-01-15

## General Guidelines
- All purchases over ₹50,000 require Manager Approval.
- Purchases over ₹5,00,000 require explicit CFO Approval.
- All hardware procurement must be consolidated through the IT department.
- Any vendor with a Trust Score below 70 must have all invoices manually reviewed by the Finance Team before payment.
- Invoices must be paid within Net 30 terms unless otherwise negotiated.
