# HR Circular: New Hybrid Work Model

**Date**: 2026-04-28
**Department**: HR / Administration

## Policy Update
Starting May 2026, TechNova is shifting to a hybrid work model. Employees will be expected in the office 3 days a week.

## Impact on Operations
Due to reduced daily headcount in the physical office, weekly team lunches and cafeteria spending will be scaled down proportionally. 
The Cafeteria budget for Q2 has been revised to reflect lower daily consumption. Vendors supplying fresh produce and dairy (e.g., FreshFarm, OneBite) will receive updated weekly quantity requirements.
